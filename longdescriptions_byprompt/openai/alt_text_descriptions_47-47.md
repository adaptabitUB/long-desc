## Chart 47: health indicators by category (00047)

### Overview and main message
This clustered column chart shows health indicators by category, measured in thousands of patients. Its purpose is to compare patient counts across three health service categories: Primary care, Emergency, and Hospitalization. The main message is a clear upward progression across the categories, with the lowest value in Primary care and the highest in Hospitalization, indicating substantially greater patient volume in Hospitalization than in the other two categories.

### Chart structure
The chart type is a clustered column chart with vertical columns, although it contains only one data series, labeled Value. The variable on the X axis is Category, with three categories: Primary care, Emergency, and Hospitalization. The Y axis represents thousands of patients and spans from 97.5 to 350.5.

### Relevant patterns, trends, and comparisons
The first key pattern is steady growth across the three categories: Primary care is 97.5 thousand patients, Emergency rises to 235.0 thousand, and Hospitalization reaches 350.5 thousand.

The second pattern is the strong contrast between the lowest and highest categories. Hospitalization exceeds Primary care by 253.0 thousand patients, showing a large spread across categories.

The third pattern is that Emergency sits close to the middle of the distribution and matches the median value of 235.0 thousand patients, making it a useful reference point between the lower Primary care figure and the higher Hospitalization figure.

### Essential key details
The maximum value is Hospitalization at 350.5 thousand patients. The minimum value is Primary care at 97.5 thousand patients. The average across all three categories is about 227.7 thousand patients, and the median is 235.0 thousand. The total across categories is 683.0 thousand patients.
