## Chart 22: health indicators by category (00022)

### Overview and main message
This clustered column chart shows health indicators across 12 healthcare categories, measured in thousands of patients, for three patient-status groups: Resolved, Under follow-up, and Referred. Its main message is that values generally decline from Primary care through to Cardiology, and that Referred is consistently the highest series in every category, Under follow-up is always in the middle, and Resolved is always the lowest.

### Chart structure
The chart type is a clustered column chart with vertical columns. It compares three series of patient status across multiple healthcare categories.

The X-axis represents the category of care: Primary care, Emergency, Hospitalization, Diagnosis, Rehabilitation, Mental health, Pharmacy, Oncology, Pediatrics, Geriatrics, Surgery, and Cardiology.

The Y-axis represents the number of patients in thousands, ranging from 54.9 to 230.8 thousand patients.

### Relevant patterns, trends, and comparisons
Across categories, all three series follow a broad downward pattern. The highest values occur in Primary care and Emergency, while the lowest values occur in Surgery and especially Cardiology.

Across all categories, the ordering is stable: Referred is always highest, Under follow-up always second, and Resolved always lowest. This indicates a consistent gap between statuses regardless of category.

The spread between categories is substantial. High-volume categories such as Primary care, Emergency, and Hospitalization are far above lower-volume categories like Geriatrics, Surgery, and Cardiology, showing a strong concentration of patients in the earlier categories listed.

### Essential key details
The overall maximum value in the chart is 230.8 thousand patients for Referred in Primary care. The overall minimum is 54.9 thousand for Resolved in Cardiology. The overall average across all 36 values is about 135.4 thousand patients.

By series, the average values are 113.4 thousand for Resolved, 134.3 thousand for Under follow-up, and 158.4 thousand for Referred. Their maximum values are 175.8, 209.3, and 230.8 thousand respectively, and their minimum values are 54.9, 58.3, and 93.2 thousand.

By category, Primary care has the highest average across the three series at 205.3 thousand patients, while Cardiology has the lowest average at 68.8 thousand. This reinforces the chart’s main message of steadily lower health indicator values as the categories progress from Primary care to Cardiology.
