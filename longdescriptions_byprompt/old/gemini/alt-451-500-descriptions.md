    The "Nord" region records the lowest sales values in every category.

    Sales in "Distribuïdor" and "E-commerce" are slightly higher on average compared to "Botiga física".

### Essential key details

    The absolute minimum sales value recorded is 64.4 M€ in the Nord region.

    The global average across all categories and regions is approximately 84.78 M€.
