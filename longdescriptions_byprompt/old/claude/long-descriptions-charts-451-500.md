## Chart 451 — CASE_04502
### Title: Operations Indicators by Category (04502)

**Overview and main message**

This chart shows order volumes across three logistics process stages for three operational performance categories. The main message is a late-drop pattern: within each category (planned, executed, incidents), values are lowest at reception and rise progressively to their peak at expedition — but in the broader operational context, the main volume decline would be expected at the final stages of a longer process chain.

**Chart structure**

The chart is a funnel chart with three data series: "Planificat" (planned), "Executat" (executed), and "Incidències" (incidents). Each series spans three process stages: reception, preparation, and expedition. The horizontal axis represents the process stages; the vertical axis measures order volume in thousands of orders (milers de comandes), ranging from 83.3 to 134.7 thousands.

**Relevant patterns, trends, and comparisons**

Within each series, values increase progressively from reception to expedition. Planned orders rise from 83.3 (reception) to 96.1 thousand (expedition). Executed orders rise from 102.0 to 113.2 thousand. Incident orders rise from 120.7 to 134.7 thousand. The three series maintain a consistent hierarchy — incidents always exceeding executed, which always exceeds planned — across all three stages.

**Essential key details**

The overall maximum is 134.7 thousand incidents at expedition. The overall minimum is 83.3 thousand planned orders at reception. The gap between incidents and planned at each stage is approximately 37–38 thousand orders, representing a consistent margin between the three operational performance categories.
