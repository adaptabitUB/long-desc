## Chart 401 — CASE_04003
### Title: Compared Profile of Health Indicators (04003)

**Overview and main message**

This chart compares the performance profiles of three health service outcome categories — resolved cases, cases under follow-up, and referred cases — across six health quality dimensions. The main message is a pattern with one dominant dimension per series: resolved and referred cases both peak strongly on accessibility, while follow-up cases peak on resolution rate — creating a consistent opposition on the accessibility–resolution axis between the two groups.

**Chart structure**

The chart is a radar chart with markers at each data point. Three data series are represented: "Resolts" (resolved), "Amb seguiment" (under follow-up), and "Derivats" (referred). Each series is scored across six dimensions: accessibility (Accessibilitat), quality (Qualitat), waiting time (Temps d'espera), resolution (Resolució), satisfaction (Satisfacció), and prevention (Prevenció). The markers make each individual score precisely identifiable alongside the overall profile shape.

**Relevant patterns, trends, and comparisons**

Resolved cases score highest on accessibility (76.5) and quality (74.7) but lowest on resolution (39.8). Cases under follow-up score highest on resolution (81.6) and satisfaction (74.4) but lowest on accessibility (45.6). Referred cases also peak on accessibility (79.1) and quality (74.0) but trough on resolution (43.3). Resolved and referred cases share near-identical profile shapes, both strong on accessibility and quality but weak on resolution — while follow-up cases invert this pattern.

**Essential key details**

The overall maximum is 81.6 for resolution in cases under follow-up. The overall minimum is 39.8 for resolution in resolved cases. Average scores across the six dimensions are approximately 60.9 (resolved), 62.9 (under follow-up), and 62.4 (referred).
